Wound/Injury Detection & Pose — Spot + Jetson Orin

Zero-shot, open-vocabulary perception works: you can type “wound” and get usable detections on a mannequin video with no task-specific training.

The standout pipeline is OWLv2 / Grounding DINO for open-vocab localization feeding Grounded-SAM2 and MedSAM for pixel-accurate wound masks — high detection rate, browser-real-time, modest VRAM.

Across all pose backends the subject’s dominant posture is classified as lying, consistent between MediaPipe, ViTPose and NLF.
