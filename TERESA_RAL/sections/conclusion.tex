\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented \ac{TERESA}, an integrated system for autonomous emergency
assessment on a legged mobile manipulator. The core contribution is a
whole-body active perception framework that treats body posture---height,
pitch, and yaw---as a first-class perceptual resource, closing the
perception-action loop at the whole-body level through confidence-gated search,
concurrent anticipatory scanning, pre-planned body reconfiguration, and an
exposure body scan with integrated injury detection. The exposure pipeline
supports all patient postures (supine, sitting, standing) and integrates
pre-trained forensic wound and burn classification models, with an interactive
web-based review phase enabling the operator to re-inspect any body region by
clicking on the camera feed.

Experiments on the physical Spot+Z1 platform demonstrated fully autonomous
emergency assessment across multiple postures with reduced idle time compared
to a sequential baseline. Future work will extend \ac{TERESA} to multi-patient
scenarios and clinical ultrasound image acquisition with impedance-controlled
probe contact.
