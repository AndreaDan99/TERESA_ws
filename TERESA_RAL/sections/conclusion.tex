\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
\draft{This paper presented \ac{TERESA}, a legged mobile manipulation
system that treats body posture as an active perceptual resource, enabling a
single platform to search, approach, scan, and perform ultrasound on an
unknown patient.}

\draft{This work shows that the manipulator arm simultaneously serves
as a tool for interaction and perception---a duality amplified by legged
posture. \ac{TERESA} exploits this coupling through posture-perception
co-design rather than explicit modelling, a practical strategy for legged
robots operating beyond structured settings.}

\draft{In future work, we would like to extend \ac{TERESA} to
multi-patient scenarios and the full \ac{ABCDE} protocol. The existing
platform can support the remaining components without architectural changes:
airway inspection and chin-lift via the impedance controller, a digital
stethoscope for breathing, a pulse oximeter for circulation, and pupillary
response analysis for disability. These steps would bring autonomous
emergency triage closer to field deployment.}
